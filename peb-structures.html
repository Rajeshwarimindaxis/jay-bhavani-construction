PEB STRUCTURES

Smart Steel Solutions For Practical Spaces.

Pre-engineered buildings (PEBs) use engineered structural components that are designed and manufactured to suit a project's requirements, then assembled at the site. They are commonly considered for facilities that need efficient construction planning and adaptable internal space.

The right solution depends on the building's use, span, clear height, loading, site conditions, local regulations and building services. Jay Bhavani Construction can discuss your requirements and help plan an approach suited to the project.

  • Industrial and manufacturing facilities
  • Warehouses and storage buildings
  • Flexible layouts and open working areas
  • Project-specific structural planning

THE DETAILS MATTER

Good Planning Starts Before Construction.

PEB design should be developed by qualified professionals around the specific project brief and applicable codes. Important items to discuss include:

Span, Height & Layout

Building dimensions, clearances, internal circulation and future use.

Loads & Structural Design

Applicable loads, support conditions, foundations and engineering requirements.

Roofing, Cladding & Ventilation

Weather protection, drainage, insulation, daylight and ventilation needs.

Access & Site Coordination

Vehicle access, loading zones, erection access and construction sequencing.
