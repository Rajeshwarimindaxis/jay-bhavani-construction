INDUSTRIAL SHEDS / WAREHOUSES

Space That Works For Your Business.

Industrial sheds are generally single-storey buildings designed for manufacturing, fabrication, workshops and other industrial operations. Warehouses focus on the storage, handling and distribution of goods, with layouts shaped around access, movement and storage requirements.

As described in the Jay Bhavani Construction brochure, these buildings can use steel or concrete structural systems and large open-plan layouts. The right solution depends on the intended use, site conditions, span, clear height, loads and required building services.

PLANNED AROUND YOUR PROJECT

The Right Structure Starts With The Right Plan.

Industrial shed planning involves more than selecting a roof and frame. The intended operation, site, structural requirements and future use all influence the design. Steel portal frames are one commonly used option for industrial and storage buildings because they can provide large, relatively unobstructed spaces.

Space & Layout Planning

Consider building dimensions, clear height, internal movement, equipment and possible future operational needs.

Structural System

Choose an appropriate structural arrangement based on span, loading, site conditions and applicable engineering requirements.

Roofing, Cladding & Ventilation

Assess weather protection, daylight, ventilation, insulation and drainage requirements for the intended use.

Site & Execution Coordination

Coordinate foundations, fabrication, erection, access and construction sequencing with the project team.
