INFRASTRUCTURE EXPERTISE

Built To Withstand The Toughest Industrial Loads.

Unlike flexible asphalt bituminous pavements which soften under hot weather and break down during heavy monsoons, Rigid Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) roads offer immense structural rigidity. They distribute heavy axle loads over wide surface areas, completely eliminating rutting, potholes, and costly seasonal repairs.

Jay Bhavani Construction manages the complete road engineering cycle: subgrade soil compaction, Granular Sub-Base (GSB), Dry Lean Concrete (DLC), Pavement Quality Concrete (PQC), steel reinforcement placement, dowel and tie bar installation, precision diamond groove cutting, and integrated roadside storm water RCC U-drains.

DURABILITY SECRETS

Precision Joint Engineering Prevents Cracking.

The true test of concrete roads is controlling thermal expansion and shrinkage. Our specialized road crews utilize calibrated joint detailing to guarantee smooth, crack-free driving surfaces.

Dowel & Tie Bar Systems

Mild steel dowel bars coated with debonding compounds transfer wheel loads across transverse joints while allowing longitudinal thermal expansion.

Diamond Blade Groove Cutting

Sawn contraction joints cut at 3mm–4mm width and 1/3rd slab depth within 24 hours of casting to guide natural shrinkage without random cracking.

Polysulphide Joint Sealing

High-grade elastomeric sealant with backer rods prevents water, dirt, and chemical ingress from penetrating to the road sub-base.

Integrated RCC Storm Drains

Precast or cast-in-situ RCC U-drains along road flanks ensure swift monsoon drainage and protect road edges from soil erosion.

SYSTEMATIC WORKFLOW

Road Construction Process

A rigorous field engineering cycle ensuring level gradients, perfect camber, and long-term pavement stability.

Alignment & Earthwork

Total station surveying, setting out road levels, topsoil clearance, and camber grading.

Subgrade & GSB Laying

Multi-stage vibro-roller compaction to 98% MDD, followed by laying and compacting GSB layer.

DLC Base Casting

Laying Dry Lean Concrete base with sensor pavers or screed vibrators, followed by curing.

PQC Paving & Texturing

Placing dowel cradles, pouring design mix concrete, needle vibration, surface float & broom finish.

Groove Cutting & Sealing

Sawing contraction joints within 24 hours, air compressor cleaning, backer rod & polysulphide sealing.
