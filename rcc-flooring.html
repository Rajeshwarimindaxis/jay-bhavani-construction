SUPERIOR FLOOR ENGINEERING

Flat, Strong & Durable. The Foundation Of Your Plant.

An industrial floor is the single most heavily abused asset in any factory or warehouse. It faces non-stop abrasion from material handling equipment, forklift tires, dynamic vibration, concentrated static rack loads, and chemical exposure. A substandard floor leads to joint chipping, dusting, and operational bottlenecks.

Jay Bhavani Construction specializes in high-tolerance industrial flooring utilizing advanced Tremix Vacuum Dewatering (VDF) systems, laser leveling technology, non-metallic/metallic mineral floor hardeners, and Steel Fiber Reinforced Concrete (SFRC). We guarantee high abrasion resistance, mirror burnished finishes, and long-lasting structural integrity.

SPECIALIZED METHODS

Flooring Systems We Deliver

From economic Tremix VDF flooring to large-scale laser screed automated warehouse floors, we tailor structural thickness, mix designs, and finishes to your operational requirements.

SYSTEM 01 • PROVEN INDUSTRY STANDARD

Tremix (VDF) Flooring

Vacuum Dewatering Process extracts surplus water from green concrete before finishing, boosting compressive strength by 25% and dramatically reducing shrinkage cracks.

SYSTEM 02 • HIGH PRECISION

Laser Screed Superflat Floors

State-of-the-art laser receiver leveling allows massive continuous single pours (up to 2,000 m²/day) meeting strict TR34 FM2 specifications for high-bay warehousing.

SYSTEM 03 • TOUGH WEAR LAYER

Non-Metallic & Metallic Hardener

Premixed dry-shake mineral aggregates troweled monolithically into wet concrete create a wear-resistant armored topping capable of withstanding steel wheel impacts.

SYSTEM 04 • JOINT-FREE ADVANTAGE

Steel Fiber Reinforced (SFRC)

Engineered steel fibers mixed directly into concrete matrix replace conventional rebar cages, delivering multidirectional tensile strength and wider joint spacing.

SURFACE EXCELLENCE

Power Troweled Mirror Finish With Armored Joints.

A superior industrial floor is distinguished by its dense, burnished finish and precision joint detailing. Our multi-pass mechanical power troweling locks the surface tight against dust and chemical penetration.

Ride-On Power Trowel Finishing

Multi-blade mechanical trowels burnish the hardener layer into a smooth, glossy, dense monolithic concrete skin that requires zero waxing or painting.

Armored Construction Joints

Heavy-duty cold-rolled steel armored joint profiles protect slab edges at high-traffic doorway thresholds from forklift wheel chipping.

Lithium Silicate Densification

Deep-penetrating chemical densifiers react with free lime inside concrete, hardening the matrix into an impermeable, dust-proof barrier.

Polyurea Semi-Rigid Joint Fillers

Flexible polyurea or epoxy joint sealants support joint sidewalls under heavy wheel traffic while sealing out moisture, dirt, and chemicals.

EXECUTION PROTOCOL

Flooring Installation Process

A synchronized pour-and-finish sequence ensuring optimal curing and flawless surface flatness.

Sub-Base & Vapor Barrier

Compacted stone base inspection, laser grade verification, and heavy-gauge vapor retarder sheet laying.

Formwork & Reinforcement

Precision steel screed rails, dowel baskets at joints, and rebar mesh / SFRC fiber dispersion check.

Concrete Pouring & VDF

High-slump design mix pour, poker vibration, surface level screeding, and vacuum mat dewatering suction.

Hardener & Power Trowel

Groove Cutting & Curing
