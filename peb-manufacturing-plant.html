RIGOROUS QUALITY WORKFLOW

The 8-Stage PEB Manufacturing Process

Every member that leaves our facility follows an audited, computerized fabrication workflow ensuring flawless assembly at your project site.

STAGE 01

3D Detailing & Engineering

Architectural plans are converted into 3D Tekla BIM models and detailed shop fabrication drawings compliant with IS 800:2007 and local wind/seismic codes.

STAGE 02

CNC Cutting & Plate Sizing

Certified IS 2062 Grade E250/E350 plates are fed into multi-torch CNC plasma machines for clean profiling of webs, flanges, base plates, and stiffeners.

STAGE 03

Hydraulic Fit-up & Tack Welding

The web and flange plates are hydraulically clamped and auto-tacked on heavy alignment jigs to form tapered columns and variable-depth rafters.

STAGE 04

Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)

Tandem SAW welding heads lay continuous, deep-penetration structural welds along both sides of the flange-web junctions simultaneously.

STAGE 05

Flange Straightening & 3D Drilling

Welded beams undergo hydraulic straightening to eliminate thermal distortion, followed by CNC drilling of splice connection bolt holes.

STAGE 06

Secondary Roll-Forming (C & Z)

High-tensile galvanized steel coils (250–345 MPa) are roll-formed into roof and wall purlins, complete with pre-punched bolt slots.

STAGE 07

Sa 2.5 Shot Blasting & Painting

Components pass through automated blast chambers to strip rust, followed immediately by airless application of zinc phosphate and epoxy primers.

STAGE 08

NDT Inspection & Tagged Dispatch

Ultrasonic and visual weld audits are performed. Every member receives an alphanumeric tag corresponding to the erection drawing for site delivery.
