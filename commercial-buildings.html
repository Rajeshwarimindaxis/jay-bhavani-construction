PLANNED AROUND YOUR PROJECT

Good Buildings Start With Good Planning.

Commercial construction requires coordination between the intended use, building layout, structural requirements, services and site conditions. Considering these factors early helps the project team make informed decisions throughout construction.

Space & Layout Planning

Consider room arrangement, circulation, entrances, accessibility and how people will use the building.

Structural Coordination

Plan the structural arrangement according to building requirements, site conditions and applicable engineering standards.

Building Services

Coordinate lighting, ventilation, electrical systems, plumbing and other services required for the facility.

Site & Execution Coordination
