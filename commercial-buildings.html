ENGINEERING PRECISION

Built From Ground Up With Structural Integrity.

A great commercial property begins below ground with meticulous soil testing and foundation engineering. Our turnkey execution covers all phases from substructure to superstructure with end-to-end quality control.

High-Strength RCC Framing

Engineered using computer-modeled structural designs, high-yield TMT bars (Fe 500D / 550D), and design-mix concrete for lifetime resilience.

Comprehensive Waterproofing

Multi-layer basement retaining wall waterproofing, terrace insulation, and wet-area treatment to safeguard against moisture ingress.

MEP & Utility Coordination

Dedicated service shafts, electrical conduit embedded routing, transformer yards, and fire-fighting pipeline integration.

Safety & Egress Compliance

Strict adherence to NBC (National Building Code) requirements for fire escapes, emergency stairwells, and accessibility ramps.

HOW WE WORK

Turnkey Execution Process

A transparent, milestone-driven civil workflow guaranteeing speed, safety, and accountability at every stage.

Site Survey & Geo-Tech

Topographical surveying, soil bearing capacity (SBC) testing, and structural coordination.

Earthwork & Substructure

Excavation, anti-termite treatment, PCC, raft/isolated footings, and plinth beam casting.

Superstructure RCC Frame

Formwork, TMT reinforcement binding, RMC pouring, and curing for columns, beams, and slabs.

Enclosure & Waterproofing

AAC block masonry, internal/external plastering, glass façade framing, and terrace waterproofing.

MEP & Quality Handover

Concealed services inspection, finishing verification, structural certification, and client handover.
